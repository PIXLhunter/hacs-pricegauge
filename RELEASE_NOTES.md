# PriceGauge 0.4.0

- Notification destinations are now configured as a shortlist in Settings.
- Each product chooses exactly one destination from that shortlist, or No notifications.
- Price-drop alerts, target-price alerts and periodic/major-change reports for that product are sent only to its selected destination.
- Removing a destination from the shortlist safely clears products assigned to it.
- Settings can still choose a shortlist destination for the Test notification function.
- Webshop quantity/pack count and ml size remain seller-specific configuration fields.
