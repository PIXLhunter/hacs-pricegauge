# PriceGauge 0.4.1

- Adds seller-specific shipping cost and optional free-shipping threshold.
- Shipping values are remembered per retailer hostname and prefilled for future products from that retailer.
- Product Min/Avg/Max, reports, graph values, target comparisons and cheapest-retailer selection include effective shipping.
- Retailer rows show product price, effective shipping, total cost and normalized comparison price.
- Historical graphs now include horizontal and vertical grid lines, value labels and date labels.
- Android Companion App notifications open `/pricegauge` when tapped and include a `Go to retailer site` URI action for the currently cheapest applicable retailer.
